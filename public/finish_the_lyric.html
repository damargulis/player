FINISH THE LYRIC

10 rounds · 40 seconds each
A few lines of a song, then a gap. Type the missing line before time runs out — Enter to answer. It is worth up to 6 pts, scored on the fraction of its words you get, so a near miss still pays; capitalization, punctuation and word order are ignored, but padding a guess with extra words costs you. Get the whole line for a speed bonus (+2 in the first 10s, +1 in the first 20s) and chain perfect lines for a streak bonus (up to +2).
